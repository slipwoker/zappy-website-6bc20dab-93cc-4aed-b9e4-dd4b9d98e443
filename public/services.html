    Transform your space with professional design expertise

    At ALEA Design, our interior design services go beyond simply selecting home and office products. We create comprehensive design solutions that reflect your unique style while maximizing the functionality of your space. Whether you're looking to refresh a single room or undertake a complete home transformation, our experienced design team brings your vision to life with meticulous attention to detail.

    Our approach combines aesthetic excellence with practical considerations, ensuring every design decision serves both form and function. From initial consultation to final installation, we guide you through every step of the design process, making it seamless and enjoyable.

    Our Design Services

    Design Consultation

    Begin your journey with a comprehensive consultation where we discuss your goals, preferences, and budget. Our designers take the time to understand your lifestyle and how you use your space, ensuring our recommendations align perfectly with your needs. This initial meeting sets the foundation for a successful design project.

    Space Planning

    Effective space planning is crucial for creating functional and beautiful environments. We analyze your space's dimensions, traffic flow, and natural lighting to develop optimal layouts. Our interior design services include detailed floor plans and 3D visualizations, helping you see the potential of your space before any changes are made.

    Color & Material Selection

    Choosing the right colors and materials can dramatically transform a space. Our designers guide you through selecting paint colors, flooring, fabrics, and finishes that create cohesive and inviting environments. We provide physical samples and mood boards to help you visualize how different elements work together.

    Furniture & Product Curation

    Beyond our own collection of home and office products, we source furniture and accessories from trusted vendors to complete your design. Our extensive industry connections allow us to find pieces that perfectly match your style and budget. We handle all ordering and coordination to ensure timely delivery.

    Project Management

    Renovations and redesigns involve multiple contractors and vendors. Our project management service coordinates all aspects of your design implementation, from scheduling contractors to overseeing installations. We ensure everything runs smoothly and stays on schedule, giving you peace of mind throughout the process.

    Styling & Installation

    The final touches make all the difference. Our styling service includes arranging furniture, hanging artwork, and adding decorative accessories to bring your design to life. We personally oversee the installation of all elements, ensuring every detail meets our high standards and your expectations.
